# Social post

Two versions. Both stay inside 280 characters, both carry the four required elements: the problem, the app, the
one action, and where to go (QR or link).

Post the poster with the text. The QR in the poster is the only way in on Xiaohongshu and Douyin, where links in the
text are not clickable.

## Chinese — Xiaohongshu / WeChat

> 每月上千张供应商发票，财务要一张张核对，一轮就是四天。
> 我们做了 ClaimPilot PRO：它自己读发票、核对订单和收货单、判断该不该放行；读不到的字段，它会说读不到，而不是猜一个填上去。
> 扫码试一张，60 秒。欢迎挑毛病。

## English — Bilibili / Weibo / LinkedIn

> A thousand supplier invoices a month, four days of review.
> We built ClaimPilot PRO: it reads the invoice, checks it against the PO and goods receipt, and clears what it can
> - and says so when it cannot read a field.
> Scan to try one in 60s. Tell us what's wrong.

## Where to post

| Channel | Link | What to post | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WeChat Moments + a course group** | clickable | poster + text + link | fastest first 50 views |
| **Xiaohongshu** | not clickable — QR only | poster + 2–3 screenshots + text | where the student audience is |
| Bilibili | in the description | the 60-second video + text | hosts the video |

Pick one to start. Do not wait for the video: reach accumulates from the day you post.

## If the page will not open

Campus and office networks block `github.io` and `github.com`. A tester scanned the poster, reached the page, tapped the
button and nothing happened — on mobile data it worked. That is the network, not the page.

Two doors, in this order:

1. **Poster QR → landing page → the feedback button.** The tester sees the product first. This is the default.
2. **`promo/qr-survey.png` → the survey directly.** The survey is on wenjuanxing, which is domestic and opens on any
   network. Use it only as the fallback for someone who cannot reach the page at all: it skips the product, so their
   answers are about the form, not about ClaimPilot.

## Posting checklist

- [ ] Poster is the 1080×1080 with a scannable QR
- [ ] The text names the problem, the app, and one action
- [ ] Test from a second phone: open the post, scan the QR, land on the page
- [ ] **Screenshot the live post with its date, the same day** — Week 9 asks for timestamped evidence
- [ ] Save the post link next to that screenshot
- [ ] If the channel strips the link, put it in the first comment and pin it

## Evidence to keep

```
promo/evidence/
  xiaohongshu-2026-11-10.png     the post, with the date visible
  xiaohongshu-link.txt           the post URL

```

The dated screenshot is what the Promotion Report calls "timestamped evidence screenshots". Taken on the day, not
in Week 10 when the post has scrolled away.
